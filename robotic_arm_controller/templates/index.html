SG90 Robotic Arm Controller

Arduino Uno R3 · USB serial · 4 independent servos

Checking Arduino…

Drag a knob along its D-shaped arc or use its slider. Each motor can be controlled independently; the Paired hands card moves L-Hand 0°→90° while R-Hand moves 90°→0°. Commands are sent after a short pause while you drag, reducing unnecessary serial traffic.
